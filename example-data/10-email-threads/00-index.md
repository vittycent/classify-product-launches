See that file for how it earns its place in this folder anyway.

Threads sit alongside the calls they bracket, extend, or contradict. Several are written parallels to something said on a recorded call — read them together, not in isolation, the same way the observation log instructs for transcripts.

## Note on length

The roster's build spec estimated roughly 11,000 words across eighteen threads. The actual total is closer to 4,900. That gap is deliberate rather than a shortfall: a booking confirmation genuinely runs to a hundred words, and a four round legal redline genuinely runs longer. Padding every thread to a uniform average would have made the short ones read as padded and the long ones read as trimmed. Length here tracks what the thread actually is, not a target.

## Note on identity

Every named person in this folder already appears in a call transcript, a CRM row, or an internal review elsewhere in `example-data/`. Nobody is invented here who does not exist elsewhere in the set.
