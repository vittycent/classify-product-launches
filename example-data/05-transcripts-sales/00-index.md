**Note on file 14.** Handelsbolaget Nord has been a customer since March 2026, so its own brand session is an expansion conversation rather than a sales call. It stays in this folder because the account executive runs it, which is normal for an upsell.

**Note on files 16 and 17.** Both are qualification calls and both are instructive for opposite reasons. File 16 never establishes who is applying the pressure, and the discovery a week later (file 03) finds a named buyer and a November deadline in ninety seconds. File 17 is a disqualification: the SDR establishes size, budget and timeline, says plainly that Rootline is the wrong fit, and books nothing.

**Note on files 18 and 19.** A discovery that reads clean and a debrief that explains why it wasn't. The champion had resigned a week before the discovery call and said so only once he had nothing left to protect.

**Note on this export.** Every file carries the auto generated summary produced by the tool, which was never reviewed by a human, and the deal notes the account executive typed afterwards. The summaries and the notes do not always say the same thing. Where they disagree, the notes are closer to what happened.
