## Notes on this export

- ARR on closed lost is the last quoted figure, not a contracted one.
- Days is from opportunity created to closed, which for two of these is wrong because the record was created late.
- Two won deals are already gone. Kryddhuset gave notice in June, Nordpack in July. Nobody has updated this view.
- Five of the seven lost rows have no note, or one word. Nobody chased them and nobody will.
- There is no field for who else was in the room.

## What this export does not contain

_Added by Sofia Bergström after the pipeline review, 2026-09-04._

This was pulled on the nineteenth. **Five deals in the current cycle closed lost after that date and are not in here:** Falkenberg Bryggeri, Bohus Charkuteri, Storhaug Meieri AS, Ödeshög Livsmedel and Kärnhuset Group. Every one of them has a recorded call with the account executive's own account of why it went, which is more than any row above gives you.

Anyone answering "why do we lose deals" from this file alone will describe a company that mostly loses to nothing in particular, because that is what the picklist says. The five in the current cycle lost for five specific and different reasons, and none of them is written down here.

**The Nordpack row is worth reading twice.** Erik flagged on the qualification call that we should not sell it, was overruled, and it churned in July. The same thing happened again last month with a packaging manufacturer, for the same stated reason, and the second one is not in this export either.
