# CSAT: onboarding and quarterly business reviews

_Example data, built for the PMM Alliance session. Fictional. Transactional CSAT, triggered automatically two working days after an onboarding completes or a QBR is held._

_Question: how satisfied were you with this onboarding / this review? 1 very dissatisfied to 5 very satisfied. One optional comment field. Sent to meeting attendees only._

## Known problems with this survey

- It only reaches people who attended the meeting. At multi brand accounts that is usually two or three people out of a project group of eight, and the person who joins for ten minutes is as likely to answer as the person who owns the project.
- Onboarding CSAT is collected while the customer is still enthusiastic and before they have used the product for a full reporting cycle.
- Ten responses. Every average on this page moves by half a point if one person answers differently, and nobody says so when the number is quoted.
- There is no field for what the meeting was supposed to achieve, so a well run meeting about a problem we cannot solve scores the same as a badly run one.
- Nobody has ever compared these scores to renewal outcomes.
- **Onboarding averages 4.67 and QBR averages 3.50.** Both Nordkost responses came from the same meeting and are two points apart, which is the most useful thing in the export and is invisible in the average.
