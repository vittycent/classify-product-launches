## What this export does not contain

_Note added by Karin Wallin, 2026-09-04._

Pulled on the eighteenth, and the cohort is defined as contracts starting between 1 July 2025 and 30 June 2026. **Six customers are therefore missing from it**, all of whom closed after the cohort window and are live accounts today: Vänerns Mejeri, Kryddgården, Fjordgruppen AS, Sydöst Livsmedelsgrupp, Lysekil Konserv and Smakbolaget. Five of them onboarded in the last four weeks.

It also still lists Kryddhuset and Nordpack Solutions, both of which are gone.

So this file understates the customer base and overstates it at the same time, and anybody counting accounts from it will get a number that was never true on any single day. It is still the best view we have, which is the uncomfortable part.

## Known problems with this file

- Health scores are missing for six accounts. They were never reviewed.
- Two churn rows have no reason at all.
- Sub vertical is a free text field in the CRM and was cleaned by hand for this export. Treat it as roughly right, not exactly right.
- Expansion and downgrade are not tracked. ARR here is the value at contract start, not today.
