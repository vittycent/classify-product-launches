**Tenure is the axis.** The onboardings are customers who closed in the weeks just before the window, so none of them appears in `../01-cohort-customers.md`, which closed in June. The check ins are three to six months in. The QBRs, renewal reviews, renewal decision, escalation, exit interview, win-back and expansion calls are established accounts, at every stage of the relationship from thriving to churned and being chased.

**Note on file 17.** The Aalborg exit interview is the single longest transcript in the set. The customer identifies the reason for churn as something Rootline chose to withhold from her rather than something it could not build.

**Note on file 18.** The win-back call for the account that churned in June. Nothing failed technically; the trigger simply disappeared, and neither side noticed a second reason was possible until it was too late.

**Note on this export.** Each file ends with the CSM's own notes written after the call. They are candid, they occasionally disagree with the account's official status, and in several cases they record a risk months before it appears in any report.
