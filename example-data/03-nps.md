# NPS: relationship survey

_Example data, built for the PMM Alliance session. Fictional. Relationship NPS sent to the named contact at every active account on 11 August 2026. Responses came in over the following four weeks and the survey closed on 4 September._

_Survey question: how likely are you to recommend Rootline to a colleague in a similar role, 0 to 10. Free text comment optional. Sent by the CS team, not by an external provider. Response rate is not tracked._

**Promoters 9 to 10. Passives 7 to 8. Detractors 0 to 6.**

## Known problems with this survey

- It goes to whoever the CSM has as the named contact, which is not always the person who uses the product and is almost never the person who pays.
- Churned accounts are removed from the send list at the point of notice, so the unhappiest customers of the last quarter are not in here at all. Kryddhuset, Aalborg, Nordpack and Sockerraffinaderiet all left before the send.
- Four accounts that onboarded during these four weeks were included anyway. They have used the product for under a month and three of them are promoters, which flatters the total.
- Two accounts returned two respondents. Repeat responses are not weighted and nobody deduplicates by account.
- The comment field is optional and roughly half are blank.
- **Nobody has cut this against the renewal watchlist.** Three of the five accounts flagged red or amber for Q4 answered, and all three are detractors.
