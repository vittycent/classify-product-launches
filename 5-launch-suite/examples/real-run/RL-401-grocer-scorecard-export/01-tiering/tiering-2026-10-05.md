**The borderline call: the case for and against, as written for Frida before she decided**

For Tier 2:

- Tier 2 tells customers and sales, and sales has a use for it this winter: an open deal wants it (Gotlandskött, grocer date 1 Dec), four prospects face spring range reviews by 28 Feb, and Nordlys's grocer scorecard is due in March.

Against:

- The loudest push for a big launch is a 2027 bet (Ida) and sales wanting it loud. The rubric counts a bet only through a goal.

## 3. Explainer for the PM

_For Nadia Rask. What product marketing takes to the product manager._

**Tier 2: tell customers and sales.** The export lets producers fill in their grocer's scorecard straight from Rootline, instead of copying figures across by hand. It sits just above the line between the two middle tiers, and Frida chose the bigger launch: she believes it deserves more noise, and the results after launch will show whether she was right.

**Why, in three reasons**

1. **Producers we don't sell to yet need it** (12 of 20). A big launch needs a market beyond the customers we already have. Gotlandskött's grocer wants product figures by 1 December; four more prospects face grocer range reviews by February; Nordlys's grocer scorecard is due in March.
2. **It moves a company goal** (10.5 of 15). A release that moves a company goal deserves more. Henrik names the template exports among what grows revenue this year, and revenue is behind.
3. **Customers were promised it** (10 of 10). Catching up still needs telling, but it is worth half of breaking new ground. The Supply package promised exports in every standard template format in November 2025.

**What would change it**

- 3 points above the line. Frida's first score on how many customers can use it (2, not 5) puts it at 50; her first score on proof (3, not 5) at 50.5; the agent's score on whether customers expect it (6, not 10) at 49.
- **Before 27 November:** if Gotlandskött signs, its grocer's 1 December date counts and the release is scored again.
- **Any time:** if Nordlys says its deal waits on this export rather than early access, it is scored again.
- **After 16 November:** the quarterly calibration scores it again with its results. That is where Frida's belief gets tested.

**Questions for you**

_Not asked before the decision. Still open, and the launch plan needs the answers._

1. Has any customer seen or tried the export? Could Gotlandskött or one of the ten accounts that asked try it before 16 November?
2. Which grocers does the 16 November layout cover, and roughly how many of our customers supply them? When do the other two templates follow?
3. Does Gotlandskött's deal depend on the export itself, or on the figures behind it? Erik asked for it early: what did Per Wallin say?
4. Has any Supply or Group customer said they expected the grocer layout because the package promised template formats?

**Now that the tier is set:** about four weeks before 16 November, so the work starts by 19 October, led by Frida with you, heard by existing customers and current pipeline. The full list is in section 6.
