## 3. Explainer for the PM

_For Jonas Wik. What product marketing takes to the product manager._

**Tier 3: tell the customers it serves, provisional.** Consolidation past four entities is what the groups have waited a year for, and it keeps the customers the groups goal depends on. It is half a point from a bigger launch, but this week brought three reasons to keep it small and precise: it doesn't fix Nordkost yet, Sydfood's expansion is on hold, and the date may move.

**Why, in three reasons**

1. **Fit with the company goals** (12 of 15). A release that moves a company goal deserves more. It is named in two: keeping the customers we win, and winning the groups.
2. **How strong the proof is** (10.5 of 15). The strongest proof short of a test: Sydfood pays for a workaround in staff time, adding brands up in a spreadsheet. 28 tickets in nine months, unchanged by the June release.
3. **Customers expect it** (9 of 10). Catching up still needs telling, but it is worth half of breaking new ground. A group product that times out on a group is not a feature gap; it is a broken promise.

**What would change it**

- Half a point below Tier 2. Frida's score on how many customers can use it (5) is open: if the fix doesn't reach Nordkost, the agent's 2 is closer, and the total drops to 46.5.
- **Week 42:** if step 3 pauses until the refresh ships, its date moves into another quarter and it is scored again.
- **Ready to test with customers:** the release stops being provisional.
- **March:** Sydfood's budget decides whether the expansion comes back, and with it the override.

**Questions for you**

1. Sofia wrote to Nordlys on 2 October that the improvement "is planned for early December" (`emails/08`). After the week 42 decision, which date can we say in writing, and who tells Sofia?
2. What do we tell Nordkost on 7 December? It raised 9 of the 28 tickets, and the fix doesn't reach its largest entity.
3. Which groups does the 7 December fix serve, by name? Support writes to each of them at Tier 3, and to nobody it doesn't fix.

**If the tier holds:** two weeks before the release, led by you with Frida's approval, heard by the groups it serves: a release note, an email to those groups, a CS briefing, an in app note, and support writing to every customer who raised it. Precisely: what it fixes, and for whom.
