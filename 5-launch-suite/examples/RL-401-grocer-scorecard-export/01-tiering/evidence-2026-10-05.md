# RL-401: Export format: grocer supplier scorecard. Evidence, before scoring

_Full path, step 1. Monday 5 October 2026. Rubric v1. The evidence for each question and both gates, strongest first, **with no scores**: Frida scores before she sees the agent's._

**The release.** "Export straight into the scorecard format the largest grocers send their suppliers." A milestone of RL-400, template export formats (45 engineer weeks). Owner: Nadia Rask. Packages: Supply and Group. Target 16 November, on track (Friday update, 2 Oct). Planned for Q2 in the December plan; slipped to Q4 because the versioned factor service ran late.

## 1. New category

_0: strengthens Rootline in food and beverage carbon accounting. 5: complementary use cases in markets next to ours (purchasing, own brand claims). 10: takes Rootline into a new category._

- An export of numbers customers already have, into the format a grocer asks for.
- Own brand claims sit next to it (Handelsbolaget, Nordlys), but the release doesn't do claims.
- The CEO calls it "the first step" towards grocers as customers (`emails/09`, 25 Sep). Today it serves suppliers, not grocers: "RL-401 ships on 16 Nov as an export. what it becomes is January's question" (Pontus, `#produkt` 28 Sep). The rubric doesn't count a bet here (section 11).

## 2. Market need

_0: only the customer who asked. 5: food and beverage companies like ours that we don't serve yet. 10: the market as a whole._

- Prospects ask: Gotlandskött (beef and lamb; the grocer's scorecard is due 1 December; wants the figures "in the grocer's own scorecard format"); Nordlys (one of its three grocers sends a scorecard, due in March).
- Answering the customer's scorecard is the reason most customers buy (use case 1; pattern P-05, 15 groups).
- Handelsbolaget, a grocer and a customer, asks for the reverse: suppliers answering in Handelsbolaget's format. "Logged, not in scope" (Friday update, 18 Sep).

## 3. Validation

_0: a gut feeling. 5: internal and external signals. 10: several kinds of data agree. Proof, strongest first: results from customers testing it; deals lost or stalled that named it; workarounds customers pay for; requests counted by customer; internal signals._

- Results from customers testing it: none yet.
- Deals: three open opportunities name it (Gotlandskött 19,000, Handelsbolaget 18,000, Nordlys 46,000). None lost.
- Workarounds: customers map Rootline's data into buyers' formats by hand (pattern P-10, 3 groups). Gotlandskött filled last year's scorecard from a slaughterhouse association's averages in a spreadsheet.
- Requests: 10 accounts on the feedback portal (FB-007), the fourth biggest idea.
- Internal: "sales will want it loud. three open deals mention it" (Nadia, 2 Oct); "the first thing I can sell to a grocer" (Henrik, 28 Sep).
- Against: no support ticket names it; "we already export to all three" common formats (Vretstorp, 31 Aug).

## 4. Strategic signals

_0: no goal, though it may be a deliberate bet. 5: supports one goal. 10: supports several goals._

- G1, grow annual recurring revenue: lists RL-400 template exports (grocers and importers).
- G3, win the groups (new multi brand and grocer customers): Nordlys is a group; Handelsbolaget's expansion is a grocer.
- The CEO's 2027 move (grocers as customers) is not a 2026 goal.

## 5. Customer request

_0: Rootline's own idea. 5: our idea, and one or a few customers asked for something like it. 10: driven by requests from many customers._

- 10 accounts on the feedback portal; Gotlandskött in its first call.
- On the roadmap since the December 2025 plan.

## 6. Market expectation

_0: not expected. 5: not expected, but customers would be delighted. 10: expected._

- The Supply package (November 2025) promises exports "in every standard template format". This closes a gap in that promise.
- Grocer templates accept spend based numbers this year, as long as the method is stated (Anders, Sörmlands call). Sörmlands went to Klimatly on that line.

## 7. Customer reach

_0: a few customers. 5: more customers, in a few segments. 10: most customers._

- Supply and Group packages. Useful to customers who supply the largest grocers, own brand or branded. Nobody has counted how many.
