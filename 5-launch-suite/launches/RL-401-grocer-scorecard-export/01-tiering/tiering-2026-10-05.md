## 3. Explainer for the PM

_For Nadia Rask. What product marketing takes to the product manager._

**Tier 3: tell the customers it serves, at the edge of Tier 2.** Exporting straight into a grocer's scorecard is something our Supply package already promises and customers keep doing by hand. Three deals name it. What keeps it from a bigger launch is that nobody has tried it yet and we don't know how many customers it serves.

**Why, in three reasons**

1. **Who needs it beyond our customers** (12 of 20). A big launch needs a market beyond the customers we already have. Prospects with a grocer deadline need it: Gotlandskött by 1 December, Nordlys by March.
2. **Fit with the company goals** (10.5 of 15). A release that moves a company goal deserves more. It sits in the revenue goal's template exports, and touches the groups and grocers we want to win.
3. **Customers expect it** (10 of 10). Catching up still needs telling, but it is worth half of breaking new ground. Our own package promises every standard template format.

**What would change it**

- 2.5 points above the Tier 2 line. Frida can move it up with the reason in section 1, or by applying the market date override for Gotlandskött's 1 December.
- A customer using it before 16 November would raise the proof and make Tier 2 clear, without either.
- Gotlandskött signing before 1 December would make its date a customer's date.

**Questions for you**

1. How many of our customers supply the grocers whose format this covers?
2. Which grocers' formats does it cover on 16 November?
3. Could a current customer try it before 16 November?
4. What do we tell Handelsbolaget, which asked for the reverse?

**If the tier holds:** two weeks before 16 November, led by you with Frida's approval, heard by the customers it serves: a release note, an email to those customers, a CS briefing, an in app note, and support writing to every customer who asked. At Tier 2: about four weeks, led by Frida with you, heard by existing customers and the current pipeline, with a blog post, a sales one pager and briefing, LinkedIn and an update to a web page.
