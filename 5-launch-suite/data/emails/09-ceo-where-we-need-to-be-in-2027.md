**Subject:** Where we need to be at the end of 2027

All,

At the board on the 11th, the question under every other question was this: now that most of our customers no longer have to report, why will they keep paying us? They also asked, again, whether food and beverage is too narrow. Here is my answer, for you to argue with before the January planning.

The obligation is moving down the supply chain. Regulators asked our customers for a report. From next year their buyers ask them for proof: grocers on own brand claims (Gotlandskött's grocer wants data by 1 December), importers on deforestation for anything with palm, soy, cocoa or coffee in it (EUDR applies to large and medium operators from 30 December), and groups resubmitting science based targets from February. None of that is a report. All of it is supplier data, which is what we are best at.

Three moves for 2027:

1. **Supplier evidence for deforestation.** Erik is selling Vretstorp palm and soy origin tracing as an add on that isn't on our price list. EUDR asks for more than origin by country. I want a discovery now and a build decision in January.
2. **Grocers as customers, not only the reason our customers buy.** Handelsbolaget pays us; Gotlandskött's grocer doesn't, yet. I want a grocer package in 2027. The grocer scorecard export in November is the first step, and I want it treated as one.
3. **Purchasing as a user.** The supplier comparison wins when purchasing is in the room. Purchasing seats belong in the 2027 price list.

And the six week look beyond food and beverage stays, because the board asked and because I want to know whether our supplier logic travels.

None of this is in our 2026 goals, because we set the goals before the Omnibus. That is my problem, not yours. I will bring 2027 goals to the January planning.

Pontus, please put the first two on the roadmap as discovery on Monday.

Ida
