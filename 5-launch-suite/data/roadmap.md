# Rootline product roadmap 2026

_Fictional. A snapshot of Rootline's roadmap as it stood on **2026-10-04** (end of 2026-W40), the last day of the Rootline data. The rows live in `roadmap.csv`, shaped like a Linear export; this page is the readable view. Generated by build scripts that are not published; read it as an export._

## At a glance

- **6 big investments**, all still in progress or discovery at the snapshot
- **51 smaller customer updates**: 33 released between January and September, 17 planned for Q4, 1 cancelled. By team: Footprint 15, Products 18, Supplier 18
- **6 milestones** inside the investments, **10 other rows**: discovery, declined, internal work and one item pushed to 2027
- **About 516 engineer weeks** of roadmap work in 2026, against an estimate of about 460 available (see `teams.md`). The plan was over full from January, which is why four of the six investments slipped
- Every row links to the customer demand behind it (`customer-demand.md`) and to the calls, emails or assets where that demand shows up

### RL-100 Interface refresh, phase 1: supplier view and reporting export

First half of the UX rebuild: a supplier view that leads with emissions, and a reporting export that carries data flags, factor versions and dates. Scope grew in Q2 to take in the export, and the two designers were shared with scenario comparison from July, so it moved from Q1 to Q4. The release target is internal only; customers have been given no date.

**Why:** Raised all year: the supplier screen's alphabetical default since March (Havsfisk), 'I still do not really understand the supplier screen' (Fjordlaks, 14 Aug), an eleven item navigation (Aalborg exit, 20 Aug), and labels customers find before Rootline does (P-13, W35 and W36). **Demand:** D-02; D-04; D-12. **Public roadmap:** DECK-3: Q1 2026. DECK-4: Now.

### RL-200 Faster data loading at group scale

Three steps. Faster imports shipped in June, and customers noticed ('The loading speed is better', Havsfisk, 14 Aug). Progress messages come next. Consolidation past four entities comes last because it needs a change to the calculation. Customers have been told it is being worked on, with no date.

**Why:** Slow loads first logged in December 2025 (P-07). A year of landings with no progress message (Fjordlaks, 14 Aug). Consolidation slow at four brands and timing out at five (Sydfood, 12 Aug, still true on 9 Sep). **Demand:** D-03. **Public roadmap:** DECK-3: Q1 2026. DECK-4: Now.

### RL-300 Brand level read only views

Give a brand manager, buyer or entity owner a view of their own brand and nothing else. Moved behind faster loading in the June re plan; design started in August and build starts in mid October.

**Why:** Asked for since spring: a purchasing manager with his own login since April (Tre Kronor, 27 Aug), a buyer asking for his own login unprompted (Lindholm, 14 Aug), and the exit that names it, 'a page that shows a brand manager his own brand and nothing else' (Aalborg, 20 Aug). Group customers hold 25 read only seats that brand managers rarely open (Bergstrand, 14 Sep). **Demand:** D-05; D-12. **Public roadmap:** DECK-3: Q2 2026. DECK-4: Next.

### RL-400 Template export formats for customers and importers

Add the grocer scorecard and importer formats customers keep rebuilding by hand. The Supply package promises exports in every standard template format, so each format closes a gap in that promise. The Products team's first half went to the versioned factor service, which ran a quarter late, so the build started in September.

**Why:** Customers map Rootline data into buyer and importer formats by hand (P-10), export buyers want it in their own scheme (Vinga, 25 Aug and 17 Sep), and a product export still shows internal material codes (Sydfood, 9 Sep). Three common formats are covered today ('we already export to all three', Vretstorp, 31 Aug). **Demand:** D-06; D-07. **Public roadmap:** DECK-3: Q2 2026. DECK-4: Next.

### RL-500 Scenario comparison: swap a supplier, see the group number

Model what the group footprint looks like if supplier A is swapped for supplier B, with data quality shown underneath. It is not reduction planning: no measure library, no cost per tonne, no pathway to a target year. Early access for a first group in November (Nordkost and Bergstrand are in). No general release date, and none has been given to customers.

**Why:** The most requested thing all year: asked for in April (Lindholm), raised by a fund and by boards (Aalborg, Kärnhuset, Nordkost), a renewal condition (Fjordgruppen), a lost evaluation ('On the roadmap means no', Storhaug, 28 Aug), and three of three W34 review and exit calls. Product chose scenario comparison over a costed abatement module in the June re plan; the head of product's written answer reached customers in August and September. **Demand:** D-01. **Public roadmap:** DECK-3: not on the slide. DECK-4: Next (in design).

### RL-600 Supplier portal v2

Rebuild the supplier side: requests in the customer's name, answers in the supplier's own format and unit, farms answering on a phone. Moved to 2027 in the June re plan so the team could take scenario comparison. Discovery runs to the end of October; small fixes ship meanwhile.

**Why:** Supplier requests spend goodwill and only work with the right signature (P-02, 13 groups); suppliers send data in formats rebuilt by hand every quarter (Bergstrand, 18 Aug); a supplier export in the wrong unit, flagged in May (Nordolie, 20 Aug). **Demand:** D-08; D-09; D-13. **Public roadmap:** DECK-3: Q3 2026. DECK-4: Later.

## Fields in roadmap.csv

| Field | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `id` | RL-001 to RL-051 are smaller updates in date order; RL-100 to RL-600 investments, with milestones numbered under them; RL-7xx discovery, RL-8xx declined, RL-9xx internal |
| `kind` | investment, milestone, update, discovery, declined, internal or later |
| `team, owner` | Footprint (Jonas Wik), Products (Nadia Rask), Supplier (Samir Haddad), or the head of product (Pontus Grahn). See `teams.md` |
| `package` | Supply, Group, both, or the supplier engagement add on (from the November 2025 packaging) |

| `planned_in_dec_2025` | The quarter in the annual plan, or `not planned` if it was added later |
| `public_roadmap` | Where it appeared on the pitch deck's roadmap slide: DECK-3 (16 March) and DECK-4 (10 August) |
| `start, target, released, declined` | Dates. Targets after the snapshot are internal and have not been given to customers. `declined` is also the date an item was cancelled |
| `slipped` | From which quarter to which, if it moved |
| `size_ew_2026` | Engineer weeks spent or planned in 2026. Milestones are counted inside their investment |
| `depends_on` | Another row that has to ship first |
| `demand` | Customer demand IDs from `customer-demand.md` |
| `signal` | Who asked, when, in their words where there is a quote. Requests from before August 2026 are described here, because the calls only cover August to early October |
| `evidence` | Files where the demand shows up, written from the top of `share/` |
