## G1 Grow annual recurring revenue

- **Why:** the Series B plan. 10.5M is the number the board holds the company to for 2026.
- **End of Q3:** 9.9M, about 0.33M of net new ARR a quarter so far. Q4 needs about 0.6M. Weighted new business and expansion closing in Q4 is about 120,100 EUR (`pipeline.md`); price uplifts on Q4 renewals add roughly 0.1M; 84,000 EUR of Q4 renewals are at risk. The board was told on 11 September that 10.2M to 10.3M is the realistic landing. Q3 ended with four wins in its last week (W40 pipeline review).
- **What moves it:** multi brand deals (12k to 45k each, at the top of the band), expansion inside groups, and renewals that don't slip.

## G2 Keep the customers we win

- **Why:** 13 of the 50 accounts that started between July 2025 and June 2026 have already churned (`example-data/01-cohort-customers.md`). Single use accounts leave after the one request that brought them.
- **End of Q3:** 84%. Five Q4 renewals flagged red or amber. Bergstrand renewed on 2 October because nobody was watching the notice date (W36 pipeline review); Tre Kronor renewed on twelve months in September.
- **What moves it:** a second use case after the first footprint (Karin's "what happens next" page, September), a second user per account, and answers for groups that want to act on the number.

## G3 Win the groups

- **End of Q3:** 7 new: Sydfood Group, Vestfold Brands, Lindholm Brands, Handelsbolaget Nord, Fjordgruppen AS, Sydöst Livsmedelsgrupp, Nordflor Brands.
- **Open:** Kärnhuset Group (tender decision 9 October), Nordlys Matgruppen (discovery, Q1 at best), Vikbolandet Foods and Tornedal Group (early).
- **What moves it:** the supplier comparison in demos, access per brand (RL-300, 2027), and an honest answer on scenario comparison.

## G4 Customers act on the number

- **Why:** "You gave us the number and then you stopped" (D-01). The board wants proof that customers use Rootline to decide something, not only to report.
- **Measure:** share of Supply and Group customers with at least one user who opened the supplier ranking (RL-008) or the supplier comparison (RL-016) in the month. Product analytics, from April.
- **End of Q3:** 23% in September, up from 11% in May. Most of the rise came after CS started showing the comparison on calls in August.
- **What moves it:** the supplier screen opening sorted by emissions (RL-034, October), scenario comparison early access (RL-501, November), the hotspots page (RL-048, December).

## G5 Marketing brings pipeline

- **Why:** almost all pipeline comes from sales outbound and from customers. The board asked in March for a channel that doesn't depend on rep hours.
- **End of Q3:** 9% of pipeline created in 2026. Of the opportunities open today, marketing content (blog posts, the supplier data guide) sourced 11% by amount, and another 34% came through the website form with no recorded source: nobody knows how those buyers found Rootline (`pipeline.md`). The first leads anyone remembers coming from the blog arrived in September (W37 pipeline review): three by the end of the quarter.
- **What moves it:** content that answers what buyers ask (the scorecard post, the brewery post, the supplier data guide), and launches that give sales something to bring.
