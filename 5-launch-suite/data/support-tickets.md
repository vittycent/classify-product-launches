# Rootline support tickets

_Fictional. Rootline support desk export, 1 January to 2 October 2026. Generated by build scripts that are not published; read it as an export._

379 tickets from 76 customer accounts, handled by the support desk (Saga Lundberg; Theo Söderlund from March) and escalated to customer success or engineering when needed. The CSV has every ticket; this page shows the themes.

**Tags.** Support tags each ticket with a theme; the `demand_id` and `roadmap_id` columns were added by Theo in September when Karin asked which tickets the roadmap would remove. Tickets with no theme are everyday questions.

**Reading the themes.** Slow imports fall after the June speed release (RL-201); consolidation timeouts at groups do not. Invitations landing in junk stop after the named sender release on 1 September (RL-031). Single sign on questions stop in May (RL-020). Questions about the supplier screen start the week the ranking shipped opening alphabetically (RL-008, 2 March) and continue until the default sort changes (RL-034).
