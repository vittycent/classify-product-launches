# Rootline launch history

_Fictional. Rootline company data, snapshot 4 October 2026. Generated by build scripts that are not published; read it as an export._

Every product release Rootline shipped from June 2025 to the snapshot, what marketing did for it, and what happened after. Rootline has never tiered its launches: the `treatment` column is what each release actually got. The 2026 rows match the released items in `roadmap.csv`; the four 2025 rows come before the roadmap file.

**Where the numbers come from.** Email rates are for the monthly release note that carried the item (or the item's own email, for a campaign). LinkedIn numbers from the company page. Adoption is the share of eligible accounts using the feature 30 and 90 days after release, from product analytics, filled in only where product tracked it. Leads and opportunities from the CRM's campaign field, which marketing fills in and sales mostly doesn't.

## What got the most and what came of it
