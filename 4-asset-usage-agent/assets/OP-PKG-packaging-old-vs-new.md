## What's changing

Until now you bought Rootline one module at a time: Core for your corporate footprint, PCF for product footprints, Supplier for supplier engagement. Every quote was built from scratch.

That made the first purchase easy and the second one awkward. Most customers who started with product footprints came back within months for the corporate footprint, because the next request asked for it. And nobody could tell from a quote what "included" meant.

From today, Rootline comes in two packages, each built around a job, with clear allowances and add ons for more.

## Old and new, side by side

| | Before 3 November 2025 | From 3 November 2025 |
|---|---|---|
| **How you buy** | Modules, one at a time, quoted per project | Two packages: **Supply** and **Group** |
| **Where most customers start** | Product footprints on their own | Product and corporate footprints together, from one model |
| **Product footprints** | Counted in each quote | Supply: 25 product models. Group: 100. A model is a recipe; pack sizes and article numbers are free |
| **Entities** | One per contract, quoted each time | Supply: 1, with more added at an incremental rate (up to 3). Group: up to 15 |
| **Supplier engagement** | A module, quoted per project | An add on for either package, in blocks of 25 active suppliers a year |
| **Users** | Not defined | Supply: 3 full, 5 read only. Group: 10 full, 25 read only |
| **Assurance provider access** | On request | Group: 2 seats included. Supply: add on |
| **Customer success** | Varied by account | Supply: a check in after onboarding, mid year and before renewal. Group: quarterly reviews |

## Which package is yours

**Rootline Supply: answer every customer request from one model.**
For food producers on the receiving end of customer scorecards and templates, with one legal entity. Product and corporate footprints, exports in every standard template format, a method note with every number, and data quality on every line.

**Rootline Group: one defensible number across the group, assured.**
For multi brand owners and large producers selling to grocers and brands. Everything in Supply, plus group consolidation across entities and systems, locked periods and restatements your assurance provider can follow, supplier declarations tracked as declared or verified, and target inventories.

**Add ons for both:** supplier engagement (blocks of 25 active suppliers a year), more product models, more entities, target inventories (Supply), assurance access (Supply), solutions engineer days in your plan, site level reviews, more users.

## What neither package does

A costed reduction pathway to a target year. Offset assessment. Packaging chemical compliance. Footprints for resold branded goods. Anything outside food and beverage. We would rather tell you now.

## If you're already a customer

Nothing changes until your renewal. Your modules keep working as they do today. Before renewal, your CSM will map what you use to the package that fits, and nothing you rely on today goes away.
