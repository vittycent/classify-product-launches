| Used for | Group: quarterly business reviews. Supply: the mid year conversation and the review before renewal |

_For CSMs: fill in every slide before the call, including the ones that make uncomfortable reading. Send the deck two days ahead. Keep the call to 30 minutes and stop on time._

---

### S01: [Customer] and Rootline: [period] review

**On the slide**

- [Date]
- Who's here: [names and roles]

**Speaker notes**

Ask at the start whether anything has changed in the organisation since the last review: a new owner, a merger, someone leaving. It changes how you read everything after it.

---

### S02: Today

**On the slide**

1. Where we are
2. What it produced, against your plan
3. What next year looks like
4. What isn't working

**Speaker notes**

Keep to the four. If the customer wants to spend the whole call on one of them, let them, and send the rest in writing.

---

### S03: Where we are

**On the slide**

- Delivered this period: [footprints, reports, submissions, with dates]
- Onboarding: [closed on (date) / still open, because...]
- Core suppliers onboarded: [x of y]
- Second person trained: [name, date]

**Speaker notes**

If onboarding is still open at a first review, say so and say why. Agree the date it closes before the call ends.

---

### S04: What it produced, against your plan

**On the slide**

| Your goal this year (your words) | What Rootline produced toward it | Where it went |
|---|---|---|
| [Goal 1, from your plan or from kickoff] | [footprints, reports, supplier data] | [customer submission, annual report, board] |
| [Goal 2] | [ ] | [ ] |
| [Goal 3] | [ ] | [ ] |

- Coverage: [x] percent of purchased goods emissions on primary data
- Restatements this period: [total, split by factor, data and method]
- Assurance or verification outcome: [findings, questions raised]

**Speaker notes**

Start from their plan, not from our output. Use the goals they gave you at kickoff, or better, the goals in their own internal plan, and show what we produced against each one. Their plan is the only thing that matters to them: a lot of value that doesn't connect to a goal of theirs won't be remembered at renewal. If an output doesn't map to any goal, either find the goal it serves or leave it off the slide.

### S07: What next year looks like

**On the slide**

- Requests we expect: [customers, templates, deadlines]
- Products to add: [number, and whether it fits the allowance]
- Reporting: [next cycle, assurance]
- Targets: [any commitments made or asked for]
- Who else in your company should see these numbers: [roles]

**Speaker notes**

The best reviews end with the customer describing a second use for the numbers, beyond the request that started the account. Ask who else inside their company gets asked about carbon.

---

### S08: What isn't working

**On the slide**

- Your list: [issues, in your words]
- Our list: [bugs, delays, anything we owe you]
- What we've fixed since last time: [ ]

**Speaker notes**

A standing item at every review. Ask it plainly and wait for the answer. If the list is empty two reviews in a row, you are probably not hearing it.

---

### S09: What we're working on

**On the slide**

| Q1 2026 | Q2 2026 | Q3 2026 | Q4 2026 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Interface refresh: supplier view and reporting export first | More template export formats | Supplier portal improvements | Interface refresh: the rest of the product |
| Faster data loading | Brand level read only views | | |
| | | | Reduction planning: exploring |

- Themes, not commitments. Order and timing can change

**Speaker notes**

Don't add dates to this slide, and don't promise anything on it. If a customer needs a date to make a decision, take the question back to product and come back with a written answer.

---

### S10: One question

**On the slide**

- If you had to renew Rootline today, would you say yes?

**Speaker notes**

Ask it plainly, then stop talking. A clear yes is worth more than any slide in this deck. Anything else, a pause, a "probably", a "we'd need to discuss it", is the most useful answer you will get all quarter: ask what would make it a yes, write down their words, and put it at the top of the actions on the next slide. Ask this at every review, not only the one before renewal.
