## 1. How to use this template

- Draft the letter with the customer during onboarding. Fill in the placeholders, pick the field set for their supplier type, and cut anything that doesn't apply
- **Decide who signs it before anything else (section 2).** It matters more than any other line in the letter
- **The customer sends it,** from their own address, in their company's name. The letter introduces Rootline as the partner that collects and processes the data, so suppliers know who they are dealing with and how their data is handled
- Ask the customer to rewrite it in their own voice. A letter that sounds like them gets more replies than one that sounds like us
- Nothing is sent until the customer has approved the final wording in writing
- Start with the core suppliers agreed at kickoff (ONB slide 11), then the next wave from the customer's plan
- The form checks answers as they come in and asks the supplier to confirm anything implausible before it is submitted

## 2. Who signs it

Suppliers answer people they know. Whose name is at the bottom of the letter decides more of the response rate than the form, the reminders or the wording.

- **The letter should come from the person the supplier already deals with:** the owner or managing director at a smaller company, the head of purchasing or the category buyer at a larger one. Not the sustainability manager, unless the supplier knows them
- **Put their direct phone number in the signature.** Suppliers who would never fill in a form will often answer a call
- If that person can't sign, ask them to send a line ahead of the letter: "You'll get a request from us this week. Please help if you can"
- Agree the signature at kickoff, with the core suppliers (ONB slide 11). Don't send the first wave without it

## 3. The letter

**Subject:** A short request about [raw material] from [Company]

> **Have your last [feed invoice] to hand before you start.**
>
> Dear [supplier name],
>
> As you may know, our customers are increasingly asking us to document the climate impact of our [products]. To answer them properly, we need to understand a little more about how the [raw material] is produced on your [farm / site / vessel].
>
> Your [farm] is at the heart of what we make, and we value the partnership we have with you. We know you are asked for a lot of information, so we have kept this short.
>
> We are asking for [six] pieces of information, listed in the form linked below. With your last [feed invoice] in front of you, it takes about twenty minutes.
>
> In return, you will receive your own [farm's] calculated figure, which is yours to keep and to use with any other buyer who asks.
>
> **Who is helping us.** We are working with Rootline, a leading carbon accounting platform built for the food and beverage industry, and trusted by food producers across the Nordics. Rootline will collect your answers through the form and process them into your figure and ours.
>
> **How your data is handled.** Your answers are stored securely in the EU and used only to calculate [Company]'s footprint and your own figure. They are never sold, and never shared with other companies or buyers. Rootline processes them on our behalf under a data processing agreement, and you can ask for your data to be deleted at any time.
>
> [Link to form]
>
> Thank you for helping us with this. If you have any questions, reply to this email and we, or Rootline, will get back to you.
>
> Kind regards,
>
> [Name of the person the supplier knows: owner, managing director or purchasing lead]
> [Title]
> [Direct phone number]
> [Company]

## 4. Field sets by supplier type

Pick one. Ask only for what moves the number.

**Dairy farm (six fields)**
1. Feed composition
2. Manure management system
3. Soil type
4. Herd size
5. Milk yield
6. Any manure treatment

**Crop grower (five fields)**
1. Crop and variety
2. Yield per hectare
3. Fertiliser type and quantity
4. Soil type
5. Irrigation and drying energy

**Fishing vessel (two fields)**
1. Litres of fuel used in the last twelve months
2. Landed weight over the same period

**Importer or trader (four fields)**
1. Country of origin, by product
2. Processing or drying method
3. Any footprint they have already calculated
4. Whether they can break it down by shipment rather than by year

## 5. Reminders

Sent automatically by the system from the customer's address. Stop as soon as the supplier replies.

**Day 7**
> A quick reminder about our request from [date]. If you have your last [feed invoice] to hand, it takes about twenty minutes: [link]

**Day 14**
> We would really value your answers. You will receive your own figure back, which you can use with any buyer who asks: [link]

**Day 21**
> Last reminder from us. If anything in the form is unclear, reply here and we'll help: [link]

After day 21, the person who signed the letter calls the suppliers that matter most. The CSM helps them decide which.

## 6. The wave two line

Add this to the letter for every wave after the first:

> [Number] of your fellow suppliers have already sent their answers back.

One sentence, and it moves response rates more than anything else in the letter.
