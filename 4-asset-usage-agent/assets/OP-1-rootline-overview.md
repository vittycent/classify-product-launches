## What Rootline does

Rootline calculates the carbon footprint of your products and your company, built only for food and beverage. We measure at the ingredient, by origin and farming practice, so the number reflects what you actually buy rather than a category average.

You get numbers you can put straight into a customer's template, with the method and sources behind every one.

## Why food needs its own tool

- **Most of your footprint is in what you buy.** Purchased goods are typically 80 to 95 percent of a food company's footprint. That is where the precision has to go
- **The same ingredient isn't the same number.** Oats from two countries, or grown two ways, carry two different footprints. A general tool treats them as one
- **Every number shows its quality.** Each line is marked as primary data, a regional average or a global average, so nobody has to guess how solid it is

## How it starts

- **Start with your first request.** For most producers that's product footprints for the customer who asked
- **About ten hours of your team's time,** at the start, from the person who knows how your products are really made on the line
- **First product footprints in about four weeks**
- **The corporate footprint comes from the same model,** so when the next request arrives you're not starting again
- **Supplier engagement when you're ready to go past averages:** a short request to the suppliers that matter most, sent in your name, with Rootline processing the answers

## What we'll need from you

- A year of purchase data, with quantities
- Your recipes or product specifications
- A list of your main suppliers

If you don't have recipes or specifications for your products, tell us early. It's the one thing we can't work around.

## Is Rootline right for you?

Probably, if you make food or beverage products and a customer, a group or a reporting deadline is asking for numbers you can defend. Probably not if most of your footprint sits outside food. We only work in food and beverage, and we'll say so if we're the wrong fit.
