## A note for CSMs and AEs (don't send this part)

- **CSMs:** go through it at the onboarding close and bring it back at every review. Each of the three things should have an owner on the customer's side and a date
- **AEs:** use it when a buyer asks what they will do with the number. It is a better answer than the roadmap slide. Show it in the demo after the first product footprint, not at the end
- **If nobody on the customer's side has time** to do any of this, it won't help them. Say so, and talk to your manager about solutions engineer days before you promise anything

---

## Three things our customers do next

### 1. Go beyond your first suppliers

Your core suppliers are in. Next, extend to the dozen or so suppliers that carry most of your number, ranked by emissions, not by spend. Each one you move from an average to their own data makes your number more true and shows you where the differences are.

**Owner:** [name]. **First wave by:** [date]

### 2. Review your sites one at a time

Your group number is the sum of your sites. Once a year, go through each site: its share of the total, its energy, its waste and the products it makes. Sites that look alike on paper rarely are, and the difference usually has a reason someone on site already knows.

**Owner:** [name]. **First review by:** [date]

### 3. Add a tiebreak rule to purchasing

When two approved suppliers are close on price, quality and reliability, the one with the lower footprint wins. Write it into your purchasing policy, so it happens in the normal course of buying rather than as a special project. It costs nothing to adopt, and it turns your numbers into decisions.

**Owner:** [name, usually purchasing]. **In the policy by:** [date]

## The longer view

Your supplier data, your history and your team's habits all build up in Rootline over time. Before you rely on that, you should know:

- **How we grow with you.** New entities, product models and supplier blocks are added during your contract, without a new procurement round
- **Where we're going.** Our roadmap themes are on one page and we review them with you at every review. We tell you plainly what we don't do yet, including reduction planning
- **Who we are.** Founded in 2019 in Stockholm, around 70 people, working only in food and beverage, with more than 50 customers across the Nordics
- **Whether we fit your plan.** Bring your programme's plan for the next three years to your next review. We'll show you where our roadmap meets it, and where it doesn't
