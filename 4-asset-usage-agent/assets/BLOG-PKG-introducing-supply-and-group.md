## 1. Two packages, each built around a job

Today we're changing how Rootline is packaged. Instead of choosing modules one at a time, you now choose between two packages:

- **Rootline Supply**, for food producers answering their customers' requests for carbon data
- **Rootline Group**, for multi brand owners and large producers who need one defensible number across the whole group

Each comes with clear allowances, and add ons when you need more.

## 2. A food industry that won't sit still

The way food companies are organised is changing faster than the contracts that serve them.

**Brands keep consolidating.** Groups buy brands, brands merge, and a company that ran one site three years ago now runs five entities on four systems. Every acquisition brings new suppliers, new products and new numbers to fold into one footprint, usually in the middle of a reporting year.

**More responsibility is landing on producers.** Customers, lenders and auditors expect producers to know what sits in their supply chain and to stand behind it. As supply chains shift, with new suppliers, new origins and new customers, that responsibility shifts with them, and it rarely arrives with extra people to carry it.

**2026 will move faster still.** Our customers are adopting AI across their businesses, making decisions faster and changing course more often. The tools they rely on have to keep up. Nobody should have to reopen a contract, or start a new procurement round, because they bought a company, launched a range or added fifty farms.

That is the reason for new packaging. It is built to flex with your business: clear allowances for what you need today, and add ons you can switch on during your contract as your business changes.

## 3. What our customers told us

Over the last year we've listened to a lot of customers, and three things came up again and again.

**The first request is never the last.** Most producers came to us because one customer asked for product footprints. Within months, a second customer, a bank or an auditor asked for the corporate number too, and they had to come back to us for a second purchase. Product and corporate footprints come from the same model, so it made no sense to sell them apart.

**"What's included?" shouldn't need a meeting.** With every quote built from scratch, it was hard to know what you were buying. Clear allowances fix that.

**Supplier engagement is real work.** Reaching farms, growers and vessels takes time on both sides, and every supplier base is different. So we now size it the way it's actually done: in blocks of suppliers, added when you're ready.

## 4. Rootline Supply

**Answer every customer request from one model.**

Supply is for producers on the receiving end of scorecards and templates. It includes:

- Product footprints for up to 25 product models
- Your corporate footprint, Scope 1, 2 and 3, from the same model
- Exports in every standard template format, with a method note behind every number
- Data quality on every line: primary data, regional average or global average
- A substantiation file when you want to put a claim on a pack
- Three full users, plus five read only logins so your sales team can answer customer questions themselves
- Implementation by a solutions engineer, and a named customer success manager

## 5. Rootline Group

**One defensible number across the group, assured.**

Group is for companies running several entities, brands and systems. It includes everything in Supply, plus:

- Up to 15 legal entities and 100 product models
- Group consolidation across entities and ERP systems, without harmonising your material codes first
- Locked reporting periods, a change log nobody can edit, and restatements split by cause
- Read access for your assurance provider
- Supplier declarations tracked as declared or verified, so you know which figures you can stand behind
- Target inventories, including the land part of your footprint
- Ten full users and 25 read only logins across the group, and quarterly business reviews

## 6. Add ons

## 8. Questions we expect

**Can I still buy product footprints on their own?**
Product footprints are the heart of Supply. We've built Supply so your corporate footprint comes with them, because that's what the next request asks for.

**What counts as a product model?**
A recipe. If you sell the same cheese in four pack sizes, that's one model with four packaging variants, not four models.

**What counts as an active supplier?**
Any supplier you send a request to during the year. They count once, however many reminders or waves they receive.

**What happens if we acquire a company during our contract?**
Add it as an entity. It joins your existing agreement at an incremental rate, so the new business can be in your footprint this year rather than after the next procurement cycle.

**I'm already a customer. What happens to my contract?**
Nothing, until your renewal. Your CSM will then help you move to the package that fits, and nothing you rely on today goes away.

**Does either package include reduction planning?**
Both include scenario modelling for single products. Neither includes a costed reduction pathway to a target year. We'd rather be clear about that than let you find out later.

**Is there a free trial?**
No. Every project starts with a conversation and a working session on your own data, which tells you far more than a trial would.
