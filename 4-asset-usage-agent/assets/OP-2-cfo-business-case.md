## 4. Why Rootline

- **Built only for food and beverage.** Most of our footprint is in purchased goods, and Rootline measures them at the ingredient, by origin and farming practice
- **A number we can defend.** Every figure traces to its source, and every line shows whether it is primary data or an average
- **We own the model.** Cheaper than a consultant from year two, and we can correct and rerun it ourselves
- **The next request starts from a base.** Product and corporate footprints come from one model, so a second customer, a bank or an auditor doesn't mean starting again
- **It leads to decisions.** Supplier level numbers show where reduction in our sourcing is possible

## 6. The risks to the value, and how we manage them

| Risk | How we'll know | What we do |
|---|---|---|
| Our data isn't ready | The working session before signing, on our own data | Fix the gaps in onboarding, with Rootline's solutions engineer |
| Nobody has time for it | Onboarding slips past six weeks | A named owner on our side, agreed before signature |
| Suppliers don't respond | Response below 25 percent in the first wave | Start with our one to three core suppliers; Rootline fills gaps with marked assumptions |
| We answer one request and stop there | No second use of the numbers by the first review | Goals agreed at kickoff and reviewed at every review, against our own plan |
| One person holds all the knowledge | Only one active user after six months | Rootline trains a second person at month six |

## 7. How Rootline supports us

- A customer success manager for the life of the contract
- Kickoff within two weeks of signature; onboarding closes when first footprints are reviewed, core suppliers are in, and there's a plan for the rest
